How v1.4 perfect-align works

1. You sing. Any language, off-key is okay — we track pitch every 50ms.

2. We analyze. BPM, key, phrases + continuous F0 curve, 12-dim chroma, energy.

3. We compose v1.4: Vocal note = top note of chord, bass follows your pitch curve at -19 semitones with glide, drums kick on your onsets (80ms window), sidechain duck when you sing loud, vocal glue octave doubling.

4. Mix & master. Demucs vocal isolation (like vocal.ai), -14 LUFS, MP3 320k.

For Mureka-level real instruments: set GENERATOR=musicgen-small in .env (needs torch+transformers, $0 self-hosted, 300M model ~1GB)
